---
title: "Fasting Glucose and Diabetes"
url: "https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/basics/getting-tested.html"
---
Fasting plasma glucose is measured after at least 8 hours without food. Normal is below 100 mg/dL. Prediabetes is 100 to 125 mg/dL. Diabetes is 126 mg/dL or higher on two tests. These thresholds come from the ADA. Symptoms include thirst, frequent urination, and fatigue. Consult a clinician for diagnosis.

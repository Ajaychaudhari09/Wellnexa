---
title: "BMI and Weight Categories"
url: "https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/obesity-and-overweight"
---
Body Mass Index is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. WHO adult categories: underweight below 18.5, normal 18.5 to 24.9, overweight 25 to 29.9, obese 30 or above. BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Muscle mass and other factors matter. Discuss with a professional.

---
title: "Blood Pressure: What the Numbers Mean"
url: "https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/high-blood-pressure/"
---
Blood pressure is recorded as two numbers. The first (systolic) is the pressure when the heart beats. The second (diastolic) is the pressure when the heart rests. Normal adult blood pressure is below 120 over 80 mmHg. Elevated and hypertensive ranges follow ACC/AHA 2017 categories. High blood pressure often has no symptoms. Lifestyle changes and medication can help. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

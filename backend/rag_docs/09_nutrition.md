A balanced diet includes vegetables, fruits, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats. Limit added sugar, salt, and saturated fat. Energy needs depend on age, sex, and activity. Calorie targets can be estimated with equations like Mifflin-St Jeor. Consult a dietitian for personalised advice.

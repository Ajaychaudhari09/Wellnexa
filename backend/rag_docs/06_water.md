---
title: "Hydration Guidelines"
url: "https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/nutrition-and-healthy-eating/in-depth/water/art-20044256"
---
Water needs vary by activity and climate. A common guideline is about 2 to 3 litres daily for adults. Thirst, urine colour, and activity level guide intake. Do not overhydrate. Consult a clinician if you have kidney or heart conditions.

# WellNexa AI — User & Clinician Manual

## 1. Patient User Guide
1. **Account Registration**: Open the app, select Register, provide your name, email, and a secure password.
2. **Recording Vitals**: Navigate to the Health tab to enter your height and weight (BMI is calculated automatically), blood pressure, and fasting blood sugar.
3. **Uploading Lab Reports**: Go to Health $\rightarrow$ Reports $\rightarrow$ Upload. Select a PDF or image of your CBC or Lipid Profile. Review the extracted parameters and tap **Confirm** to sync them with your permanent health timeline.
4. **Managing Medications**: In the Track tab, add your active prescriptions. Tap **Take Dose** daily to track adherence and receive automated refill warnings when stock runs low.
5. **AI Health Guidance**: Ask questions regarding meal timing, vital trends, or lifestyle in the AI Guide tab. The assistant will cite clinical guidelines and reference your active medications while redirecting acute symptoms to emergency hotlines.
6. **Sharing with Doctor**: Open Profile $\rightarrow$ Doctor Sharing. Select a duration (e.g. 24 Hours) and scope. Present the generated QR code to your doctor. Tap **Revoke** at any time to immediately disable access.

## 2. Healthcare Professional Guide
1. **Accessing Patient Records**: Scan the patient's QR pass or open the secure link provided during the consultation.
2. **Reviewing Clinical Summary**: View the patient's longitudinal vitals, active medications, documented allergies, and confirmed lab reports on a single consolidated screen.
3. **Recording Consultation Notes**: Enter chief complaints, clinical observations, and recommended action plans at the bottom of the portal. Tap **Save Consultation Note** to automatically sync it with the patient's Health Timeline.

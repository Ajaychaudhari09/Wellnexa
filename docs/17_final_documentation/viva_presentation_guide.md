# WellNexa AI — MCA Capstone Viva Presentation Guide

## 1. Presentation Structure (10 Minutes)
- **Minutes 0-2 (Problem & Motivation)**: Explain health fragmentation, medication non-adherence morbidity, and unverified AI chatbot risks.
- **Minutes 2-4 (Engineering Architecture)**: Showcase the Modular Monolith in FastAPI, PostgreSQL 16 + `pgvector`, and Flutter cross-platform client.
- **Minutes 4-8 (Live Demonstration Story)**:
  1. Login with demo account (`patient@wellnexa.ai` / `Password123!`).
  2. Walk through the **Intelligent Health Dashboard** (Medicines checklist, vitals, nutrition progress, adherence card).
  3. Show the **Medical Report OCR & Confirmation** workflow and timeline synchronization.
  4. Demonstrate the **Grounded AI Health Assistant** with active medication recognition and emergency triage redirection.
  5. Demonstrate **Doctor Time-Bound Sharing** and instant revocation kill-switch.
  6. Download the complete **JSON Health Archive** from the Privacy Center.
- **Minutes 8-10 (Technical Defense & Q&A)**: Highlight the 23 passing test suites, STRIDE threat model, and sub-150ms latency benchmarks.

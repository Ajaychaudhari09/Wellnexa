# WellNexa AI — UI/UX Design System & Tokens

## 1. Color Palette Tokens
- **Primary Brand (Deep Teal)**: `#0F766E` (Represents clinical credibility, balance, and vitality)
- **Primary Light (Cyan)**: `#14B8A6` (Interactive buttons, selected states, progress bars)
- **Primary Dark**: `#115E59` (App bar accents, dark mode surfaces)
- **Secondary (Clinical Blue)**: `#3B82F6` (Vitals, hydration, metric trends)
- **Accent (Energy Amber)**: `#F59E0B` (Calorie metrics, nutrition highlights)
- **Status Alerts**:
  - `Success`: `#10B981` (Normal biomarkers, taken doses, adherence $\ge 85\%$)
  - `Warning`: `#F59E0B` (High/low physiological boundaries, refill warnings)
  - `Error`: `#EF4444` (Critical flags, missed doses, emergency warnings)
- **Neutral Background**: `#F8FAFC`
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Primary**: `#0F172A`
- **Text Secondary**: `#64748B`

## 2. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Display / Headers**: Inter / Roboto Bold (24-28sp)
- **Card Titles & Section Headers**: SemiBold (16-18sp)
- **Body Text**: Regular (14sp, 1.4 line-height)
- **Captions & Flags**: Medium (11-12sp)

## 3. Accessibility Standards
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliant contrast ratios ($\ge 4.5:1$ on text).
- Minimum tap targets: $48\times 48\text{dp}$.

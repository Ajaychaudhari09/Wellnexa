# WellNexa AI — Existing System Analysis

## 1. Apple Health / Google Fit
- **Target Audience**: General consumers using smartwatches and mobile devices.
- **Strengths**: High-frequency automatic biometric collection (heart rate, step counts, sleep cycles).
- **Limitations**:
  - Closed ecosystem (Apple Health restricted to iOS/watchOS).
  - Weak medical report OCR capabilities; does not parse multi-panel blood tests into structured longitudinal databases.
  - No integrated interactive medication inventory management or quantitative monthly adherence scoring.
  - No time-bound granular doctor sharing pass with instant revocation.

## 2. Practo / 1mg / Apollo 247
- **Target Audience**: Telemedicine consultation and pharmaceutical commerce users.
- **Strengths**: Large doctor directory, online prescription fulfillment, and lab test booking.
- **Limitations**:
  - Primary focus is transactional commerce rather than holistic long-term health management.
  - Lacks grounded AI health assistance over combined lifestyle, nutrition, and laboratory reports.
  - Health records are stored in siloed vendor portals with limited exportability.
  - Zero integrated mental wellness journaling with automated crisis safety hotline integration.

## 3. MyFitnessPal / Cronometer
- **Target Audience**: Fitness enthusiasts and dieters tracking calories and macronutrients.
- **Strengths**: Extensive crowdsourced food database and barcode scanning.
- **Limitations**:
  - Completely decoupled from clinical medical records, lab reports, and doctor consultations.
  - No medication tracking or drug-allergy context.
  - Heavy monetization and advertising cluttering the user interface.

## 4. Ada Health / Babylon (Symptom Checkers)
- **Target Audience**: Users seeking automated triage and diagnostic symptom checks.
- **Strengths**: Probabilistic diagnostic reasoning engine.
- **Limitations**:
  - High clinical liability risk; attempts disease diagnosis rather than empowering patient health literacy.
  - Lacks longitudinal tracking of verified laboratory blood reports or prescription adherence.
  - No clinician collaboration portal or patient data export controls.

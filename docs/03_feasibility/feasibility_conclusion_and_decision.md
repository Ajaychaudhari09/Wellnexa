1. **Technical**: Flutter, FastAPI, PostgreSQL + pgvector, and RAG architectures are fully validated, high-performing, and cross-platform capable.

3. **Economic**: Entire project development and academic deployment achievable at **$0 cost** using free-tier cloud infrastructure.
4. **Legal & Safety**: Clear non-diagnostic boundaries, emergency triage redirection, and GDPR/DISHA consent alignment protect patient safety and mitigate clinical liability.
5. **Risk**: All 6 identified core risks have concrete mitigation strategies and automated contingency fallbacks.

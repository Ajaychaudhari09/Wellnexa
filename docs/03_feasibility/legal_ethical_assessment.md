# WellNexa AI — Legal, Ethical & Safety Assessment

## 1. Regulatory Alignment (DISHA, GDPR Article 9, HIPAA)
- **Data Minimization**: Only health metrics and documents explicitly submitted by the user are stored.
- **Purpose Limitation**: AI processing is strictly limited to patient educational summaries and personal trend visualizations.
- **Consent & Portability**: Users can toggle off AI analysis, view immutable access logs, and export their full personal health record archive at any time.

## 2. Medical Device & Diagnostic Boundaries
- WellNexa AI is classified as a **Personal Health Management & Educational Software System**, not a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) diagnostic tool.
- All generated outputs include explicit disclaimers:
  > *"WellNexa AI provides evidence-based educational health insights for informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for clinical judgment, medical diagnosis, or personalized treatment."*

## 3. Ethical Guardrails & Crisis Intervention
- Direct requests for disease diagnosis, prescription alteration, or acute emergency treatment are strictly blocked and redirected to emergency medical hotlines (112, 102, 911).
- Detection of severe mental distress or self-harm keywords triggers automatic attachment of verified 24/7 crisis support resources (Tele-MANAS, KIRAN).

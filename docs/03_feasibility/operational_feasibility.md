# WellNexa AI — Operational Feasibility Analysis

## 1. User Usability & Mental Model
- **Patient Experience**: Intuitive 5-tab bottom navigation (Home, Health, Track, AI Guide, Profile). Clear interactive checklists for medicines, single-tap dose confirmations, and plain-language report summaries eliminate complex medical jargon.
- **Clinician Experience**: Clean read-only clinical dashboard showing longitudinal vitals, active medications, allergy warnings, and confirmed lab tests on a single page, minimizing physician review time during consultations.

## 2. Process Integration & Workflow Feasibility
- Does not disrupt clinical workflows: Doctors do not need an account or software installation to review patient data—they access authorized records via a secure time-bound link or QR code scanned directly on their consultation device.
- Patients retain complete sovereign ownership of their data with instant access revocation.

## 3. Operational Feasibility Verdict
**FEASIBLE**: Highly intuitive for end users of varying technical literacy; zero operational friction for healthcare providers.
